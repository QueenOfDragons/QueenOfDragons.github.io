About me

Vittoria.

I'm a senior IT consultant with over 30 years of experience in data, mainly in banking, financial services and insurance. I work with SAS® (a software platform widely used by companies to manage and analyse large amounts of data): not just analysis, but developing procedures and applications with all its components. I've been a certified SAS instructor since 1992 and have taught courses in Italian, English and French.

I've led small teams, followed projects from requirements analysis through to checking the results, and also worked in healthcare, public administration and territorial analysis with geographic information systems.

Recently I've started designing and building my own applications with the help of artificial intelligence (Claude and ChatGPT), which I use as a development team working under my direction. For each project I decide the idea, the rules, the look and what it has to do; the assistants help me write the code.

What I can do

  • Development with SAS and all its components, and SAS training (certified instructor since 1992). Find out about SAS consulting
  • Data analysis, Data Warehousing (large company data stores) and data quality
  • Managing IT projects and small teams
  • Geographic information systems (GIS): data linked to maps and territory
  • Conceiving a digital product and defining its features
  • Designing games and quizzes that engage an audience
  • Directing the development of an application with artificial intelligence
