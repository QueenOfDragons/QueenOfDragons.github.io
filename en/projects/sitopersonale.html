Io, in ordine (Me, in order)

A personal organiser: everyday life arranged in sections, all in one place.

Try the demo (in Italian)

The real program contains personal data and runs only on my computer. The demo is a demonstration version with made-up data: feel free to try it, whatever you do stays only in your browser. The demo interface is in Italian.

What problem it solves

Instead of lots of scattered Excel files, notes and reminders, everything is in one place, with a home page showing the sections and a monthly planner made of coloured notes, like Post-its.

The sections

  • BussolaLavoro (Job Compass): tracks the job search: applications with their status and a log of updates, interviews, text templates, documents, comparison between offers.
  • Gestione spese (Expenses): expenses, deadlines and instalments, invoices to collect, budget, summary for the accountant; it imports Italian electronic invoices automatically.
  • Benessere (Wellbeing): habits with "gentle goals", mood diary, exercise, medicines with stock levels, reminders for medical appointments.
  • Formazione (Learning): courses and certifications in a numbered path, study hours with a stopwatch, skills map, ready-made text for the CV in Italian and English.

Strengths

  • New sections can be added without touching the rest: the home page finds them on its own.
  • Every section exports to Excel and PDF; one-click backup.
  • No online account and no paid services.
  • Calm colours and no endless pages to scroll.

Technologies

Python (a small server, with no libraries to install), pages in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, a single SQLite database (one file holding all the data). Reading electronic invoices and creating Excel files are written from scratch.

My role

I decided what each section has to do and how it should look. The code was written with the help of Claude.
