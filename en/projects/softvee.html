Softvee

Softvee's presentation website: web applications, games and interactive quizzes.

Visit softvee.it

What it is

A single-page website presenting Softvee, which designs web applications, games and interactive quizzes and experiments with artificial intelligence in development. Its stated approach: "from prototype to product".

What it contains

An introduction, the QuizArena page, the "What we do" section and the privacy policy. It links to Softvee Hub, the entry point to QuizArena.

Technologies

A static website, i.e. plain HTML and CSS pages (the language of web pages and the language of their graphics).

My role

I designed the layout of the site, chose the technology to use and wrote all the texts. I conceived all the icons and images, using ChatGPT to produce them.
