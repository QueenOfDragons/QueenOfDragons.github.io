AgoraWord

Word and logic games for live social streams, where the audience plays by typing in the chat.

What problem it solves

People who stream live on social media need to keep their audience engaged. AgoraWord turns the stream into a game: viewers type their answers in the chat and the program recognises who guessed right and awards points in real time.

Who it's for

Content creators who stream live. It was created for my own streams as ElleVee and is genuinely in use, with an audience that keeps coming back to play.

What it does

About fifteen games, including:

  • Hint Game (guess the word from the clues), Hangman, Secret word, Mastermind
  • Flags, Country shapes, Map
  • Word chain, Word ladder, Decode, Impossible definition
  • True or false, Guess the number, Emoji rebus

To engage the audience: overall leaderboard, tournament mode and competition by match days, badges for the winners (for example the "champion" of the week with a dedicated graphic theme), thank-yous to donors, animated scenes. Interface in four languages.

How it works, in short

It's a Windows program that installs with one click. Inside there's a small server that receives the messages from the live stream chat. The game content (words, clues, sentences) is loaded from Excel files.

Coming soon: AgoraWord Web

A version to play in the browser, with nothing to install. The first game is Hint Game, in a single-player version, with its own graphic style: a puzzle-solver's notebook, with coloured sticky notes, notes taped on with sticky tape, and rubber stamps.

Technologies

React (to build the interface), Vite (prepares the code), Electron (turns the website into an installable program), Express (the small built-in server), Framer Motion (animations). For the web version also TypeScript and Phaser (a game library).

My role

I conceived all the games. The code was written under my direction with the help of ChatGPT and Claude.
